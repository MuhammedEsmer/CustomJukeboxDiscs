# Privacy and copyright

Uploading sends the selected audio file, title, player UUID, and player name to the connected
Minecraft server. The file becomes available to modded clients that are near a jukebox playing
its programmed disc. Files are not sent to any external service by this mod.

Only upload audio you are authorized to share. Server operators are responsible for moderation,
storage retention, backups, and applicable copyright rules. Client cache files are stored under
`.minecraft/customjukeboxdiscs/cache` and may be deleted while Minecraft is closed.
